Status and scope

What these colors mean

Current government/faction identity claims are substantially HERALDRY_DERIVED and RESEARCH_REQUIRED. The exact hexadecimal values shown here are UI_ADAPTATION choices and remain PROVISIONAL.

Military identity is tracked separately. In Military mode, finished cards show supported reusable palettes; technical blueprints show documented variation that prevents one unified palette; sketch blueprints show insufficient evidence.

Rasalhague note

Culturally informed UI adaptation

Free Rasalhague uses a Swedish-inspired blue and gold/yellow UI direction, informed by its documented Swedish/Scandinavian cultural identity. Cyan/light blue and blue-gray remain supporting colors from the heraldry-derived identity.

This is not a claim that Swedish blue and yellow are canonically specified as official Free Rasalhague Republic national colors.
