What these colors mean

Source evidence and identity conclusions are tracked separately from reusable display palettes.

Government/Faction and Military identity are separate evidence contexts. Exact hexadecimal values are UI adaptations unless a source explicitly specifies a digital value. Unresolved Military cases remain visibly unresolved rather than receiving invented source colors.
