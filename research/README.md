# Research

Research records preserve what a source says or shows before interpretation is promoted into a canonical record.

Each claim should identify its source, locator, observation or quotation, evidence classification, interpretation, confidence, and approval status. Artwork observations must distinguish colors observed in an image from colors interpreted as faction identity colors.

`claims/remaining-identities.yaml` records the 41 additional Camo Specs faction/index identities researched in the expansion pass. Camo Specs page and displayed emblem evidence is recorded separately from the provisional identity interpretation; unresolved cases remain `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` without assigned UI colors.
